\section{What transfers beyond this contest}
\label{sec:transfer}

Our numbers belong to one text, one host type and one recipe family and should not be reused elsewhere, but five
practices should carry over to other contests that charge wall-clock time. (i)~Rehearse the exact artifact, cold: it
predicted official scores more accurately than any cheaper proxy we built (MAE \val{holdout-mae} on the final
uploads), at the price of a full run (\cref{sec:oracle}); harnesses should compile every graph before the clock
starts (\cref{sec:traps}). (ii)~Price every change in the charged resource, and measure
that price with a short sweep of the time budget (\cref{sec:price}). (iii)~Measure the noise floor before believing a
lever (\cref{sec:seeds}). (iv)~Expect anything chosen by a noisy score to give back part of its edge
(\cref{sec:salts}). (v)~Use a surrogate as a noise filter, not a search engine (\cref{sec:simval}); pooled runs from
several teams would let it say more (\cref{sec:contrib}).
